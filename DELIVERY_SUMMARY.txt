API ENDPOINTS:
================

Available on http://localhost:5080:

Health:
  GET /                 - Service status
  GET /api/admin/status - System info

Audio-to-Sign:
  POST /api/audio-to-sign/upload-audio      - Upload .wav audio
  POST /api/audio-to-sign/upload-video      - Upload video
  POST /api/audio-to-sign/text-to-signs     - Text to signs
  GET /api/audio-to-sign/get-sign-image/... - Sign images

Mentor Dashboard:
  GET /api/<email>/dashboard/users           - Students list
  GET /api/dashboard/users/<id>/summary      - Student summary
  And 12+ more endpoints for student/mentor management
